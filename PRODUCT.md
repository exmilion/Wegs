# Sat4Life — Product Context

## Product

Sat4Life is a public satellite imagery gallery and monitoring dashboard for CICESE (Centro de Investigación Científica y de Educación Superior de Ensenada). It displays real-time satellite pass data and imagery captured from polar-orbiting weather satellites (Meteor M2-3, M2-4, NOAA 15/18/19) via SatDump, stored in Supabase, and served through a React web app.

## Users

- **Primary:** Researchers, students, and staff at CICESE who monitor satellite passes and review imagery
- **Secondary:** General public interested in satellite imagery from Baja California
- **Context of use:** Quick glances at a desk (monitor), occasional deep review of a specific pass on laptop, rare mobile check from the field

## Brand

- **Name:** Sat4Life
- **Tone:** Scientific precision meets space-age wonder. Not playful, not corporate. Confident, calm, informative.
- **Visual identity:** Deep navy backgrounds evoking the night sky, cyan accents inspired by satellite signal displays, the pelican logo as the sole organic element
- **Anti-references:** Not a SaaS dashboard. Not a generic tech startup landing page. Not a weather widget. Not a military operations center.

## Strategic principles

1. **Data first.** The imagery is the hero. Every design choice serves making satellite photos immediately visible and explorable.
2. **Status at a glance.** Users should know system status (live/demo), latest pass info, and satellite activity within 2 seconds of landing.
3. **Scientific credibility.** Timestamps in UTC and local time, satellite names, pass counts. Precision matters.
4. **Spanish language.** All UI copy is in Spanish. This is a Mexican research station.
5. **Minimal chrome.** The interface should feel like a window into space, not a control panel with excessive decoration.
